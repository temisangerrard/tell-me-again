Copy, paste, send. Edit the first line per person.

---

Hi [name], I made an app for keeping your family's stories: you ask a parent, grandparent or sibling a question, record the answer, and keep it. I'd like you to try it for a week and tell me what's wrong.

**Open it:** https://tellagain.app
It works in the browser. On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" so it opens like an app.

**What to try**
1. Pick one person you'd like to hear from (a parent is a good start).
2. Sit with them and tap Start. The app suggests what to ask. Ask it out loud, let them answer, tap Next.
3. Or call them from the app and record the call.
4. Listen back to what you got.

**Two things to know**
- Your recordings stay on your phone, not on a server. If you lose or reset the phone they are gone, so go to Family > Your information > Download full backup once you have something worth keeping.
- To sign in you give an email address and we send a six-digit code. That's all we keep.

**Tell me what's wrong.** Family > About > "Tell us what's wrong" opens an email to me with your phone details filled in. Anything counts: confusing words, a button that did nothing, a moment you gave up.

Thank you. Temisan, Settley Studio
